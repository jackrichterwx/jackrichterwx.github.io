About Me
======
I am a 4th year PhD candidate in the Department of Climate and Space Sciences and Engineering at the University of Michigan working with Professor Claire Pettersen in the [Cold Precipitation Processes Group](https://pettersen.engin.umich.edu/). My research is focused on investigating snow and ice microphysics using remote sensing and in situ observations from ground-based, airborne, and spaceborne platforms.
